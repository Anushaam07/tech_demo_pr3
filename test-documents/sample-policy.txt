Company Policy Document - Employee Handbook 2025

Section 1: Remote Work Policy
Employees are allowed to work remotely up to 3 days per week. Remote work requests must be approved by direct managers at least 24 hours in advance. All remote workers must maintain availability during core hours (9 AM - 3 PM local time) and respond to messages within 2 hours.

Section 2: Paid Time Off
Full-time employees receive 15 days of paid vacation per year, accrued monthly. Sick leave is provided separately with 10 days annually. Employees must submit PTO requests at least 2 weeks in advance for vacation periods longer than 3 days.

Section 3: Data Security
All employees must use company-approved VPN when accessing internal systems remotely. Passwords must be changed every 90 days and meet complexity requirements (minimum 12 characters, including uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters). Sharing login credentials is strictly prohibited and may result in termination.

Section 4: Performance Reviews
Performance reviews are conducted quarterly. Employees receive feedback from direct managers and peers. Annual bonuses are determined based on performance ratings, with top performers receiving up to 20% of base salary.

Section 5: Professional Development
The company provides $2,000 annually per employee for professional development courses, certifications, or conferences. Employees must obtain manager approval before enrolling and provide completion certificates for reimbursement.
